# References

Outside material that informs the engine's design.

## Molecular Matters (Stefan Reinalter)

Author of the Molecule Engine, a commercial, closed-source engine; its design
is documented on the blog.

- Blog: [Molecular Musings](https://blog.molecular-matters.com/), engine
  internals: memory, job system, data-oriented design, handles.
  - [Generic, type-safe delegates and events in C++](https://blog.molecular-matters.com/2011/09/19/generic-type-safe-delegates-and-events-in-c/):
    allocation-free delegates and events, cited by *Game Programming Patterns*
    (Observer).
- GitHub: [MolecularMatters](https://github.com/MolecularMatters)
  - [raw_pdb](https://github.com/MolecularMatters/raw_pdb): C++11 reader for
    PDB debug files.
  - [psd_sdk](https://github.com/MolecularMatters/psd_sdk): C++ reader for
    Photoshop PSD files.
